# Code of Conduct

We want FOLTIO to be a welcoming, productive project. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to **hello@folt.io**. Maintainers will review reports fairly and keep details confidential where possible.
